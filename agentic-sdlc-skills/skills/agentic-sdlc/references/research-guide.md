# Research Guide

## What to research

### UI research
- page layouts
- navigation patterns
- mobile responsiveness
- common states: empty, loading, error, success, no results
- interaction patterns for forms, dashboards, detail pages, and search

### Architecture research
- framework options
- data fetching patterns
- auth approaches
- database choices
- background job patterns
- deployment strategies

### Competitive research
- features competitors consistently have
- workflow shortcuts users expect
- UI conventions that reduce learning friction

## How to capture findings

Never dump raw links and raw thoughts only. Write:
- what pattern was found
- why it matters here
- whether to adopt or reject it
- what tradeoff it creates
